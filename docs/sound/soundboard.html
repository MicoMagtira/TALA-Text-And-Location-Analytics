TALA SOUND BOARD

Every sound here is made live by your browser from four 8-bit voices: two pulse waves, a triangle and a noise channel. On top of that sits a starlight echo, a soft dark delay that gives TALA its night-time space. Nothing is recorded, so the set is original and adds almost nothing to the app's size. Press a button to listen, mark it Keep, Tweak or Cut, then copy your notes at the bottom.

Scenes

Hear the sounds together, the way they follow each other in the app. Start here to judge whether the set feels like one family.

Interface

Short and quiet on purpose. A data app reruns a lot, and these play many times in every session, so they should feel like soft glints, never chirps that grab attention.

Analysis

What the models are doing. A compute starts, results arrive, a pipeline step finishes, or the answer came straight from the cache. Warnings and errors are soft and low, never a harsh buzzer.

Celebrations

The night-sky moments: the language gate, the shooting-star START, the constellation that forms when TALA is ready, and the fanfares for finishing a track. They resolve from A minor (night) up to C major (first light).

Page signatures

One short sound per page. It plays when that page's main result is computed fresh, in place of the generic "Results ready". Cache hits play the quick "Instant" cue instead. The Text track uses plucky pulse voices (words); the Geo track uses bells and drops (places on a map).

Music

Looping night-time tracks, one per area of the app. Only one plays at a time. Today TALA streams a 7 MB MP3 loop. All five of these together are a few KB of code.
