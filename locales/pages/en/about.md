--- key: aikasalig
### AIKasalig
TALA comes from **AIKasalig**, the project *Advancing Responsible AI in Philippine Healthcare: A Multi-Year NLP-Driven Roadmap for Trust, Transparency, and Ethical AI*.

Its goal is an **AI Trust Toolkit**, multilingual and informed by communities. The toolkit uses natural language processing (NLP) in five languages (Filipino, Cebuano, Ilocano, Hiligaynon and English) to bring out what the public says about AI in health care: signs of trust, sentiment, and health misinformation.

The project's outputs include annotated text collections (corpora), classifiers, dashboards, an **AI Trust Index**, and **AI-literacy modules** for health workers and communities. The work is aligned with the digital-health priorities of DOST-PCHRD.

--- key: program
### Part of a DOST-NICER program
AIKasalig is Project 4 of the program **Research and Development Center on Natural Language Processing for Health Applications** (RDC-NLPHA). The program is part of DOST's **NICER** (Niche Centers in the Regions for R&D) and is implemented by **National University, Manila**.

--- key: tala
### Where TALA fits
TALA is a hands-on **learning module** of AIKasalig. It teaches the text-and-location analytics behind toolkits like this one: reading the place a comment names, checking it against the GPS point, comparing sentiment and aspects place by place, and finding hotspots. It works on health-service comments, in the same five languages as the project.
